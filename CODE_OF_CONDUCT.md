# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation welcoming and free from harassment. Be respectful, assume good intent while examining claims rigorously, disclose conflicts, protect private information, and focus criticism on work rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and offer an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when possible. A dedicated conduct contact will be named before public launch.
